Bulk Order Quote — Hostel Bunker Cots & Single Cots (50+ Pieces)

Furnishing a hostel, PG, dormitory or institution? Bunkworks supplies steel bunker cots (double decker beds) and single cots factory-direct from Kerala. Orders of more than 50 pieces get a separate bulk quote — with pricing, delivery and timing confirmed in writing.

Quick answer

To get a bulk quote for hostel beds, send Bunkworks the number of bunker cots and single cots, any custom size, your delivery city and opening date via the form below, WhatsApp (+91 90724 31550) or email (bunkworksindia@gmail.com). Orders above 50 pieces receive a written bulk quote.

How a bulk order works

  1. Send your requirement — quantities, sizes, delivery city and opening date.
  2. Get a written quote — factory-direct per-piece price, delivery cost and timing.
  3. Confirm the order — payment and delivery terms are agreed in writing.
  4. Delivery and assembly — beds arrive flat-packed with legs and platforms separate; assembly is bolt-together.

The number of bunker cots and single cots, any custom size, your delivery city, whether the site has stairs or a lift, your opening date and whether you also need mattresses.
