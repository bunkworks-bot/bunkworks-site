Hostel Beds Manufacturer in Kerala

Bunkworks manufactures galvanised steel hostel furniture in Kerala, India. Our core products are bunker cots / double-decker beds and steel single cots for student hostels, working-professional PGs, dormitories, staff accommodation and institutions.

Hostel beds made for high-use spaces

For hostel projects, the useful comparison is not only price. Frame material, corrosion protection, deck construction, load rating, assembly and replacement of parts all affect long-term value. Bunkworks uses galvanised steel frames with epoxy coating and 12mm plywood bases on its core bed products.
